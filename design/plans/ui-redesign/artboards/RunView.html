Accessibility

  • Numbers in black circles (1) are the keyboard focus order; the focused control shows the 3 px focus ring.
  • A control people cannot use is aria-disabled, not disabled: it stays in the tab order so a screen reader reads its reason, and the reason is also printed under it (on touch, under the menu item), never only in a tooltip.
  • No colour-only status: every pill and outcome has an icon and a word (allowed, refused, waiting, dry run).
  • Reduced motion: under prefers-reduced-motion the live dot does not pulse, skeletons do not shimmer and toasts appear without sliding; toasts with a link stay until dismissed.
  • Contrast is checked in light and dark for text, pills and focus rings (tokens of the design system).

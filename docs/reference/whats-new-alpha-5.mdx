<Warning>
  Yukta is still alpha. The workflow JSON, REST API, MCP tools and plugin contract can change before 1.0.
</Warning>

Alpha.5 adds notifications: Yukta now tells people what happened and asks them for what it needs, in one place.

- **Inbox**: a bell with a count, toasts, an Inbox page with three tabs, filters and the answer form, and deep links (`/inbox?item=<id>`). A resolved question stays under *Resolved* with who answered. See [How notifications work](/concepts/notifications).
- **Desktop**: operating system notifications from an open tab, raised only when it is hidden and your level for the category is `DESKTOP`; `yukta-cli notify` does the same without a browser. See [Notifications from the terminal](/guides/operate/notifications-from-the-terminal).
- **Preferences**: a level per category and muted sessions, kept per named key.
- **Alerts**: runs that fail or finish, runs held by a supervisor and deliveries that give up raise notifications; a workflow raises its own with `NOTIFY_TERMINAL` and `target: inbox`.
- **Channels**: one `NotificationChannel` SPI and one outbox replace the input responders. See [Configure notifications](/guides/operate/notifications) and [Write a notification channel](/guides/extend/write-a-notification-channel).
- **CLI and MCP**: `yukta-cli notifications`, `yukta-cli notify`, `list_notifications` and `mark_notifications_read`.
- **Plus**: notifications and queued deliveries are kept in PostgreSQL.

## Breaking changes

All in one place; the steps are in [Migrate to alpha.5](/reference/migration-alpha-5).

- `yukta.input.responders.*` is refused at start-up; use `yukta.notifications.channels.webhook.<name>.*`.
- The `InputResponder` SPI is replaced by `NotificationChannel` (SPI version `1.1`).
- The metric `yukta.input.delivery` is now `yukta.notifications.delivery`.
- A webhook body gains a `notification` object; the alpha.4 fields stay.
- The `KV_STORE` and `SESSION_MEMORY` plugins are removed.
- Plus: a migration adds the notification tables and drops `input_delivery`.
- `/api/notifications/**` is open to keys limited to sessions; they see only their sessions' items.
